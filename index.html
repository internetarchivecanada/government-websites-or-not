Government websites… or not?

Is it run by a government, or not? Guess for ten websites at a time, drawn from the Internet Archive’s list of US and Canadian government websites and shown as the Wayback Machine saved them.

Every site here came from the October 2026 review of the Internet Archive’s government-websites list, where 6,843 addresses turned out not to be government. Links open the site’s history in the Wayback Machine.

A game from Democracy’s Library, the Internet Archive’s free collection of government information from local, regional and national governments.

Meets WCAG 2.1 success criterion 2.1.4, Character Key Shortcuts: the G, N and C answer keys can be switched off on the start and results screens.

An experiment from Democracy’s Library at the Internet Archive; not an official government publication.
